## Stasis for unu 2.0.7

- Diagnostic reports include recent logs, app version and available Android crash information.
- View trip history and the Bluetooth SMS inbox on compatible scooters.
- Improved battery identification and registration guidance. Registration remains disabled until its service is available.
- Biometric authentication keeps the app locked with a retry option when authentication is cancelled or fails.
- Keycard identifiers retain their correct display order.

Available features depend on the scooter hardware and installed firmware.
